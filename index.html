Every person is unique, and so is every process

First comes listening: to what is already there in your ideas, your habits and your curiosity. From there, we support you in developing a personal daily practice. It can combine different things and change along the way.

Berlin is a hub of musical experimentation, with hundreds of active sound explorers. Album Lab helps you find the right person to support your practice, within a program that leads you toward a finishing line.

For some, the final result may be a leftover, for others, a vinyl record. It doesn't really matter. The focus is on a process that feels alive and true.

How it works

Personalized curriculum

After an in-depth conversation, we design a curriculum just for you, drawing on a wide network of Berlin-based artists who can spark your process.

One-to-one mentoring

Personal sessions to support your daily practice, online or in person.

Group meetings + lectures

We meet in person, in a moderated conversation where everyone shares ideas, work in progress and challenges.

Lectures are designed for the group, some given by guest artists.

The six months

  1. Month 1 Preparing the ground

    Setting up a daily practice. We coach you to make it a routine that is both fluid and focused.

  2. Months 2–3 Planting

    Connecting your practice to recording, listening and editing.

  3. Months 4–5 Watering

    Developing your material: listening, refining, narrowing down.

  4. Month 6 Harvest

    Choosing, finishing and giving your album a physical form, then celebrating with a release event.

Who it's for

Beginners and experienced people alike: what matters is curiosity, not level.

The one requirement is commitment: six months and a daily practice.

Team

Album Lab is run by Manuel Pessoa de Lima and Chloë Sobek. We also invite guest artists and lecturers, chosen together with the group around what each person brings.

Manuel Pessoa de Lima

Performer-composer creating genre-defying, humorous performances with piano, electronics and spoken monologues. He holds a doctorate from CalArts in the Performer-composer program, and has released three albums, the latest on vinyl by Black Truffle Records. He has performed at festivals and venues across Europe and the Americas, including Café Oto and Oscillation Festival.

Instructor Dina Maccabee

Composer and performer from California, based in Berlin.
