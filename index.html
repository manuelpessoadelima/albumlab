A laboratory of music exploration guiding participants through the making of an album.

Don't really fit in a conservatory?

There are other ways to learn music.

Album Lab is a six-month program to develop your own creative process, together with a small group of people doing the same.

Instead of a diploma, you finish with a printed CD of your music.

How it works

For six months, the program alternates between two kinds of weeks:

Week A · One-to-one mentoring

A personal session to support your daily practice. Online, or in person if you are learning an instrument.

Week B · Group meeting + lecture

Three hours in person, in Berlin. First, the group shares ideas, processes and challenges. Then a lecture on a topic chosen for the group, sometimes with a guest teacher.

The six months

  1. Months 1–2 Preparing the ground

    Setting up a daily practice, without the pressure of recording yet.

  2. Months 3–4 Planting

    Connecting your practice to recording, listening and editing.

  3. Months 5–6 Harvest

    Narrowing down, choosing, and finishing.

  4. The end Release

    Printing your CD and celebrating with a release event.

Every person is unique, and so is every process

Album Lab is not a production school that teaches you "how to make an album". There is no single right way to make one.

Before the "how to" comes listening: to what is already there in your ideas, your habits and your curiosity, so it can grow in its own way.

From there, you develop a daily practice of your own, and we build a frame around it. It can combine different things and change along the way. Finding it is part of the process.

For whom

Beginners and experienced people alike: what matters is curiosity, not level.

What it does ask for is commitment: six months, and a daily practice.

TEACHERS

Album Lab is run by a small core team of artists. Around it, we invite guest teachers and lecturers chosen for each group: instrument teachers, sound artists, programmers, producers, researchers, whoever your practice calls for.
